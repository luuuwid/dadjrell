Frequently Asked Questions

Event Hosting

Do you accept bookings outside Region 8?

Yes! While we’re based in Kananga, Capoocan, and Palompon, we’re open to hosting and voice-over bookings outside Region 8, subject to availability. Travel and accommodation are arranged based on your location.

How much does hosting cost?

Hosting fees depend on the type of event, event duration, venue location, and program requirements. Message us for a tailored quote.

How do I book a host for my event?

Message us with your event type, date, venue, and program details. We’ll confirm availability and send you a quote.

Do you provide a script or program flow?

Yes, we can help prepare the program flow and script. You can also send us your own and we’ll work from it.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for peak seasons like weddings, fiestas, and pageant months. Last-minute requests are accepted subject to availability.

Can we meet or talk before the event?

Yes! We’re happy to go over the program, details, and special requests ahead of time.

Do you require a down payment for event hosting?

Yes, a 30% down payment is required to reserve your date. The remaining balance is settled on or before the event.

What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy for events?

Rescheduling is allowed subject to availability. The down payment may be non-refundable. Please message us as early as possible if your event details change.

Voice-Over

Do you accept pre-recorded voice-over requests?

Yes! Pre-recorded voice-overs by Host Dadj are available for events, pageants, promotional materials, and other productions. Send us your script and project details, and we’ll confirm the timeline and fee.

Can you do live voice-over at my event?

Yes! Host Dadj can provide live voice-over for events, pageants, and programs. Message us with your event details and date to check availability.

How do I request a voice-over?

Message us with your script, project type, preferred deadline, and where the voice-over will be used. We’ll reply with a quote and timeline.

How long does it take to receive my voice-over?

Turnaround depends on script length and project requirements. We’ll confirm the delivery timeline once we receive your script.

Can I use the voice-over commercially?

Usage depends on the agreed scope, such as social media, radio, TV, or events. This is why our fee varies by scope of usage.

Do you require a down payment for voice-over projects?

Yes, a 30% down payment is required to start your project, and the balance is due upon delivery of the final file. Cancellations are allowed before recording begins, but once recording has started, the down payment is non-refundable.

Do you require a down payment for live voice-over?

Yes, a 30% down payment is required to reserve your date. The remaining balance is settled on or before the event.

What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy for live voice-over?

Rescheduling is allowed subject to availability. The down payment may be non-refundable. Please message us as early as possible if your event details change.
